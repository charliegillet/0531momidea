# Copy-Paste Templates

Grab-and-go scripts. Replace `[brackets]`.

---

## Confirmation text (send right after a "yes" on the phone)
> Hi [Name], great talking to you! Confirming: I'll build [Business]'s website —
> [pages/features, e.g. "1-page site with your services, hours, photos, map, and a
> click-to-call button"]. Flat **$[X]**, due only once it's live and you approve it.
> I'll send a preview link soon. — [Your Name], [phone]

---

## Preview-ready text
> Hi [Name]! Your website draft is ready 🎉 Take a look: [link]
> Let me know any changes. Once you're happy, we go live and it's $[X]. — [Your Name]

---

## Payment-due text (after approval)
> Awesome, glad you like it! Total is $[X]. You can pay via [Venmo @/card link/check].
> As soon as that's in I'll point it to your domain and you're officially live. Thanks [Name]!

---

## Follow-up (no response after 3–4 days)
> Hi [Name], just circling back on the website draft I sent — [link]. No rush, no cost
> to look. Happy to tweak anything. Want me to make any changes?

---

## Review / testimonial ask (after going live + paid)
> Thanks again [Name]! If you've got 30 seconds, a quick Google review would mean a lot
> and helps your site show up too: [review link]. And if you know any other local owners
> who could use a site, send 'em my way — I'll take great care of them.

---

## Cold email (when they say "send me info")
**Subject:** Free website preview for [Business]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for your time! As promised — I build clean, mobile-friendly websites for local
> businesses, and you don't pay until it's live and you love it. Flat $[X], no monthly fee
> from me.
>
> Here are a couple I've made: [link], [link].
>
> If you send me your logo + a few photos (or I can pull them from your Facebook/Google),
> I'll put together a free draft this week. Want me to start?
>
> — [Your Name]
> [phone] · [your site]

---

## Elevator pitch (memorize this)
> "I build simple, professional websites for local businesses, and you don't pay until
> it's done and you're happy. Flat price, no risk."
